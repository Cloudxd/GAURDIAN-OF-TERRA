Practical Energy-Saving Tips

1

Smart Thermostat

Install a programmable thermostat to automatically adjust temperatures based on your schedule and preferences.

2

LED Lighting

Replace traditional bulbs with LED lights that use 75% less energy and last 25 times longer.

3

Unplug Devices

Eliminate phantom power drain by unplugging devices when not in use or using power strips with switches.

4

Seal Air Leaks

Caulk and weatherstrip windows and doors to prevent heated or cooled air from escaping your home.

5

Water Heating

Lower water heater temperature to 120°F (49°C) and insulate hot water pipes to reduce heat loss.

6

Appliance Efficiency

Choose ENERGY STAR certified appliances and run full loads of laundry and dishes to maximize efficiency.
